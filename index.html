Sailing Games

Two small browser games about sailing with real-world winds. They work on phones and computers.

Belladonna’s Journey Sail the yacht Belladonna anywhere in the world, riding the trade winds and the Roaring Forties, and resupply at 29 ports before her 50 days of food and water run out. Westward Passage Cross the North Atlantic from Falmouth to the American east coast on the NE trade winds.
