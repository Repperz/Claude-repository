Cast off from Falmouth

Sail your yacht across the North Atlantic and make landfall anywhere on the green stretch of US coast, from Florida to Maine.

  • The wind flag shows where the wind comes from and how hard it blows.
  • You can't sail closer than 35° to the wind. Point higher and you stop dead "in irons".
  • Set the sail to suit the wind angle: pulled in when sailing upwind, let out when the wind is behind you. Keep the marker in the green band.

Tip: the westerlies blow against you near Britain. Sailors since Columbus have gone south to the Canaries first, then turned west and let the NE trade winds carry them across.
